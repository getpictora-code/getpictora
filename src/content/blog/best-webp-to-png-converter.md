## Why Convert WebP to PNG?

While WebP offers superior compression for websites, **PNG** remains the gold standard for editing and printing due to several key factors:

* **Full Software Compatibility:** Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, and older editing software handle PNG files seamlessly.
* **Transparent Backgrounds:** Converting WebP back to PNG preserves alpha-channel transparency without creating black or white box fills.
* **Lossless Quality:** PNG retains crisp lines, text clarity, and sharp edges for graphic assets.
